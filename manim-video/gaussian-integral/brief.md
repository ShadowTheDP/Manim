# gaussian-integral

Create a production Gaussian Integral episode in Traditional Chinese mathematical narrative: derive I = integral over R of e^(-x^2) dx by squaring the integral, converting to a double integral, changing to polar coordinates, separating theta and r, evaluating both parts, and concluding I = sqrt(pi). Include visual demonstrations of the Gaussian curve area, the squared plane, radial symmetry, and polar coordinates. Production output must be 1080p60.
